# Hack Lab: if something is not working

Print this page and keep it in the box lid. Write the grown-up's number at the
bottom by hand; it is not printed here on purpose.

## The screen says "AI helper is away"

Nothing is broken. The AI helper needs the internet; the tricks and the sliders
do not. Pick a trick from the list, or change the numbers in one. The AI helper
comes back by itself when the internet is back.

## The page will not open on the iPad

1. Check the small computer (the Hack Lab box) has its red light on. If not,
   unplug its power and plug it back in. Wait about one minute.
2. On the iPad, open Safari and go to `http://hacklab.local/`.
3. If that does not open, look at the WiFi list on the iPad. If you see a
   network called **hacklab-setup**, the Hack Lab lost the home WiFi. Join
   hacklab-setup, and a page opens by itself. Pick the home WiFi and type its
   password. The iPad then goes back to the home WiFi on its own, and
   `http://hacklab.local/` works again after a minute.

## It says the box is "not plugged in"

Check the USB cable between the Hack Lab and the box, at both ends. Try the
other USB socket. The page notices within a second or two.

## It did a trick and now the box acts strange

Press **Undo last change**. If it is still strange, press **Back to how it
came**. Those two buttons always put it right. Nothing you try can break it.

## The screen says "Uncle is helping"

That means the grown-up is looking at the same screen from far away to help.
Nothing to do. It switches off by itself after two hours.

## When to message the grown-up

- The red light is on, but `http://hacklab.local/` still does not open after
  the steps above.
- The page opens but every trick fails.
- Anything smells hot or looks broken. Unplug the power first.
